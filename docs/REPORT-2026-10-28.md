What r4 got right that r3 did not: the Macedo checklist and the Alcácer file-naming list are no longer called elements
(the new noise kinds worked). What r4 still gets wrong, all the same failure:

- the four real memórias (Valongo loteamento, three Cascais loteamentos) are called "not an element";
- the OA minuta of the author's termo is called "not an element" (r3 had it right).

Diagnosis (read the prompts, not the scores): the reader sees the first 3,500 characters of each file. For a 30-page real
memória that is a cover page plus a long dotted table of contents, which looks exactly like the checklist and file-list
noise it was just taught to reject. The Valongo memória opens as a letter ("Resposta ao ofício n.º …"). The OA minuta
extracts as one word per line and opens with "A presente minuta … transcreve a redação prevista …". None of those shapes
existed in the training expedientes.

Key fix: the Valongo area key said "49 977,00 m2"; every value in the data set is written without its unit (the unit is in
the question), so the key was out of convention. Fixed in generator/stranger.py and sealed/stranger-test.jsonl (one
line). Rescored on the fixed key: r3 5/7, r4 7/7 on D2 value. No training data touched.
